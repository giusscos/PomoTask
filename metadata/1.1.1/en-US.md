## Highlights

### Dial drag during sessions
Adjust focus or break time mid-session by dragging the pomodoro dial while paused. Turn dial drag on or off from the timer menu. Remaining time updates live as you wind, then resume when you’re ready.

### Improved watchOS app
- Tomato red focus / break chrome aligned with iPhone
- Progressive Timer mirrored with iPhone, widgets, and Live Activity — play, pause, and reset stay in sync
- Struggle escape on Watch (keep going, shorten, or break)
- Classic timers redesigned for the wrist with wall-clock accuracy
